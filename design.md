# Nova Supremo — Design System

## Brand
- **Nome:** Nova Supremo
- **Localização:** Nova Iguaçu, Santa Eugênia
- **Tom:** Sofisticado, premium, sensorial
- **WhatsApp:** 21920018580

## Color Palette
- Background primary: `#0d0618` (deep dark purple/black)
- Background secondary: `#1a0a2e` (dark purple)
- Surface: `#251040` (purple surface card)
- Accent primary: `#8b2fc9` (vibrant purple)
- Accent bright: `#c084fc` (light purple)
- Gold accent: `#d4af37` (premium gold)
- Gold light: `#f5d67b` (gold highlight)
- Text primary: `#f5f0ff` (near-white)
- Text muted: `#a78bca` (muted purple)

## Typography
- Display/Hero: `Playfair Display` — serif, elegant, premium
- Headings: `Raleway` — clean semi-serif, modern
- Body: `Lato` — readable, clean
- Accents/Logo: `Playfair Display Italic`

## Layout
- Full-width sections with dramatic hero
- Dark backgrounds with glowing purple/gold accents
- Overlapping elements, asymmetric layouts
- Noise/grain texture on backgrounds for depth
- Generous spacing, cinematic feel

## Motion
- Fade-in stagger on scroll for sections
- Smooth hover scales on cards
- Subtle glow pulse on CTAs

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- White backgrounds
- Rounded pastel cards
- Generic food icons
- System fonts
